Internal Security FAQ

Q: Where do I report a suspicious email?
A: Forward it as an attachment to security@internal.example, don't just
forward it inline, since that can strip the headers we need.

Q: Can I use my personal laptop for work?
A: Only for email and calendar via the approved mobile-device-management
profile. No source code, customer data, or internal documents on personal
devices.

Q: What do I do if I lose a work device?
A: Report it within 1 hour through the IT portal or the emergency line if
after hours. The device will be remotely wiped once reported.

Q: How often do I need to rotate my password?
A: Every 90 days for systems that don't support hardware security keys. SSO
sessions backed by a hardware key are exempt.
