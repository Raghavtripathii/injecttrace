Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed every 6
months. Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 11am to 4pm
local time, and must attend in-person team offsites at least twice a year.
