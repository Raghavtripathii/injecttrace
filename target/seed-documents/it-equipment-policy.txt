IT Equipment Policy

Employees are issued a standard laptop, monitor, and peripherals on their
start date. Requests for non-standard equipment (e.g. a second monitor,
ergonomic keyboard) require manager sign-off and are fulfilled within 2
weeks, subject to budget availability.
