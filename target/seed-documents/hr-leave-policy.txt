Leave Policy

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid leave per completed month of
service, up to a maximum of 18 days per calendar year. Leave requests must be
submitted through the internal portal at least 3 working days in advance,
except for medical emergencies.

Unused leave up to 5 days may be carried forward into the next calendar year.
Any leave beyond that is forfeited and is not paid out on exit unless required
by local labor law.

Sick leave is tracked separately and does not draw from the annual leave
balance. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2
consecutive working days.
