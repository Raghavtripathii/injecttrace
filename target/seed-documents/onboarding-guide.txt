New Hire Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the team. Your first week covers three things: account setup,
introductions, and your 30-60-90 day plan.

Day 1: IT will provision your laptop, email, and access to the internal
document portal. HR will walk you through benefits enrollment.

Week 1: You'll be paired with a buddy from your team who is not your manager,
specifically so you have someone low-stakes to ask "obvious" questions.

By day 30: You should have a written 30-60-90 day plan reviewed by your
manager, covering ramp-up goals, a first small deliverable, and any training
you still need.

All new hires get read access to the shared documents used across teams,
including this onboarding guide, the leave policy, and the expense
reimbursement policy.
