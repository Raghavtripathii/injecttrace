Leave of Absence Policy

Employees requesting an extended leave of absence (beyond standard annual
leave) must submit a written request to HR at least 4 weeks in advance,
except in documented medical emergencies. Approved leave of absence periods
do not accrue additional annual leave, but existing accrued leave is not
forfeited during the absence.

Returning employees are guaranteed reinstatement to the same or an
equivalent role, provided the leave of absence did not exceed 12 months.
